A puzzle about getting out

10 Levels of Hell

A man wakes at the bottom of hell. The stairs out are ten levels up. Every time he gives up and starts a level over, the attempt he abandoned stays behind as a shade and walks it again beside him.

Every level is proven solvable with exactly the shades it gives you and impossible with one fewer. The clocks are set to the fastest escape there is: one wasted step and you are out of time.
